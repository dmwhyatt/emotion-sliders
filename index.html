Seven musical variables

Make one piece of music sound happy, sad, scary, peaceful or neutral using seven faders — tempo, sound level, articulation, phrasing, register, timbre and attack speed.

An interactive version of the production experiment in Bresin & Friberg (2011), Cortex 47, 1068–1081.

Run the experiment

Twenty short pieces — four scores × five emotions, in random order. For each one you get an emotion (e.g. happy) and you move the seven faders until the music sounds like it. The faders stay where you leave them when you move to the next piece, as in the original study. Only your final fader positions are scored.
