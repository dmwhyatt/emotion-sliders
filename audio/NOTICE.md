# Sample attribution

The samples in this folder were extracted from the SoundFont **MuseScore_General_HQ v0.2** (`MS Basic.sf3`), shipped with MuseScore 4 and released under the MIT licence -- see `SoundFont_License.md`.

Only four presets were used (grand piano, French horn, flute, trumpet), converted to mono FLAC. Generated by `tools/build_bank.py`.
